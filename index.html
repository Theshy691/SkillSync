Is this the score an employer will see?

No. SkillSync provides an estimate against the job description you supply. Employers use different screening methods. The score is guidance for your application, not a hiring prediction.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The sample workspace runs a local keyword comparison and labels its results as a demo. Sign in to use the AI comparison and save your scan history.

What happens to my resume?

The text-only demo compares documents in your browser. In signed-in mode, document text is sent to the analysis service, and a resume snippet and comparison are saved to your account history. Image extraction may use the server. Remove sensitive details you do not want to submit.
