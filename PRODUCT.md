## Platform

web

## Overview

SkillSync is an AI-powered job application acceleration suite and ATS (Applicant Tracking System) intelligence platform designed for software engineers, tech professionals, and career switchers. It helps candidates bridge the gap between their resumes and target job listings with precision keyword matching, XYZ-formula bullet tailoring, cover letter generation, interview question synthesis, salary benchmarking, job pipeline tracking, and resume credibility auditing.

## Primary Users & Jobs To Be Done

- **Active Job Seekers & Tech Candidates**: Candidates applying for software engineering, frontend, backend, DevOps, and cloud roles who need high-signal resumes tailored specifically to individual job descriptions.
- **Career Switchers**: Developers transitioning stacks or seeking to identify missing competencies and get personalized 4-week learning roadmaps.
- **Hiring Managers & Recruiters (Verification Persona)**: Evaluators who need to fact-check resumes for technological anachronisms, metric hyperbole, and unsubstantiated buzzword stuffing.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Autonomous ATS Matcher**: Real-time multi-dimensional scoring (Overall, Technical Competency, Role Relevance, Experience Depth, Skill Density) comparing candidate resumes against job descriptions.
2. **AI Action Suite**:
   - 1-Click Resume Tailorer using Google's XYZ impact formula (`Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]`).
   - Personalized Cover Letter & Speedrun Application Kit generator.
   - Interactive Technical Interview Simulator with Taglish & multilingual support.
   - Salary Benchmarking & AI Negotiation Coach with percentile brackets.
   - 4-Week Skill Gap Learning Roadmap with actionable milestones.
   - Company Intelligence & Interview Dossier generator.
3. **Resume Credibility Radar & Anti-Hallucination Audit**: Detects timeline anachronisms (e.g. claiming 10 years of Kubernetes before 2014), hyperbolic metrics (>1000% speedup), and keyword stuffing.
4. **Smart Job Discovery Engine**: Live aggregator pulling verified opportunities with 1-click ATS workspace transfer.
5. **Skill Vault**: Multi-document candidate profile synthesizer extracting verified credentials across resumes, certifications, and portfolio projects.
6. **Multi-Platform Job Application Pipeline**: Kanban tracking board for applications (Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected) with celebratory confetti.

## Voice & Tone

- **Voice**: Crisp, technical, encouraging, authoritative, and direct.
- **Avoid**: Fluffy career advice clichés, overly corporate jargon, and generic AI templates.

## Architecture & Constraints

- **Frontend**: Responsive Single Page App (SPA) built with semantic HTML5, DM Sans typography, Tailwind CSS utility classes, custom CSS design tokens, and modular vanilla ES6 JavaScript.
- **Motion & Audio**: Linear/Raycast-grade spring animations (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), rolling score odometer, SVG radar charts, and opt-in Web Audio API tactile feedback.
- **Backend**: Containerized Apache/PHP 8.2+ API services with Gemini AI integration and rate-limiting guardrails.
- **Database & Auth**: Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS) policies and REST API integration.
